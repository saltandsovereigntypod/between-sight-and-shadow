A different way of paying attention

Your body is not failing a test.

Vision is more than clarity on an eye chart. It is also how the eyes aim, focus, move, work together, and help the brain make sense of space.

When those tasks demand more effort than they should, reading, screens, driving, shopping, balance, and concentration may become exhausting. Chronic illness and hypermobility can add further layers of pain, fatigue, sensory strain, and uncertainty.

Between Sight & Shadow offers plain-language education, practical accommodations, and spiritually inclusive tools that support self-observation and informed conversations with qualified clinicians.

A note from the threshold

This site does not treat vision conditions, Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, pain, or mental health conditions. It helps people notice patterns, reduce unnecessary strain, prepare for appointments, and build gentler rituals around the realities of their bodies.

You may belong here if

Ordinary tasks cost more than other people realize.

  • Reading causes headaches, blur, losing your place, or unusual fatigue
  • Busy stores, scrolling, or fluorescent lighting overwhelm you
  • Your balance or sense of where your body is in space feels unreliable
  • You live with hypermobility, EDS, chronic pain, or fluctuating capacity
  • You need accommodations but struggle to explain why
  • You want spiritual practice that respects disability and consent
  • You are tired of being told to push through symptoms
  • You want language for what you notice without self-diagnosing

The guiding principle

Healing is not forcing the body to behave.

It is learning its language, gathering trustworthy support, and making room for a life that does not require you to abandon yourself.
