Simple by design

Privacy notice

This version of Between Sight & Shadow does not include advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or a production account system.

Accessibility preferences

Text size, contrast, spacing, font, motion, decoration, reading-ruler, and focus-mode preferences are stored in your own browser using local storage. They are not transmitted to Kate.

The Body Ledger demonstration

The current Body Ledger is a front-end demonstration. Entries are stored only in the browser being used and are not encrypted. Do not enter real medical records, identifying health information, or anything you would not want another user of the same device to see. Clearing browser data may permanently delete entries.

A future production version will require secure authentication, database access controls, deletion and export tools, and an updated privacy policy before real health information is collected.

External links

Articles link to outside sources for further reading. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Between Sight & Shadow does not control them.
