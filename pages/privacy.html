Privacy notice

This starter website does not include advertising trackers, analytics, user accounts, or cookies used for marketing.

The text-size and high-contrast controls store only your display preference in your own browser using local storage. That information is not transmitted to the website owner.

If the contact form is connected to Formspree or another service, information submitted through that form will be processed by that provider and delivered to the website owner. The form should not be used to submit medical records, protected health information, emergency messages, or highly sensitive personal information.

If analytics, email marketing, digital sales, appointment scheduling, or other third-party tools are added later, this notice must be updated to identify the data collected, purpose, providers, retention practices, and available privacy choices.

Before launch: Add a contact email for privacy requests and update this page after choosing hosting, analytics, mailing-list, payment, or scheduling providers.
