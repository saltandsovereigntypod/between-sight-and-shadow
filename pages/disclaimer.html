Seek individualized care

Symptoms can have many causes. Persistent, worsening, sudden, or disruptive symptoms should be evaluated by an appropriate licensed professional. Do not begin, stop, or change medication, prescribed exercises, lenses, medical devices, or treatment based on this website.

Sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a curtain or shadow in the visual field, sudden double vision, new flashes with many floaters, neurological symptoms, loss of consciousness, chest pain, or other potentially urgent symptoms require prompt medical assessment.
