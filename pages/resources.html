Practical tools

Resources for noticing, preparing, and adapting

Use these prompts as organizational tools, not diagnostic tests.

01

Visual Strain Log

Track the task, duration, lighting, distance, symptoms, severity, and recovery time. Patterns can help you give a clinician more precise information.

VisionAppointmentsPrintable idea
02

Appointment Preparation Sheet

List your three most disruptive symptoms, when they began, what worsens or relieves them, medications, relevant diagnoses, prior eye care, and the questions you need answered.

Self-advocacyMedical care
03

Visual Environment Audit

Review glare, contrast, font size, screen position, reflections, flicker, task distance, posture, clutter, and whether supplies are within easy reach.

HomeWorkAccommodation
04

Capacity & Recovery Map

Identify early warning signs, common overexertion triggers, helpful supports, expected recovery needs, and tasks that can be simplified or delegated.

PacingChronic illness
05

Accessible Ritual Builder

Choose the purpose, then select a posture, length, sensory level, tool, opening action, central action, and closing action that fit today’s capacity.

WitchcraftLow energy
06

Questions for a Vision Evaluation

Ask which areas were assessed, what conditions were ruled out, whether symptoms could involve binocular vision or focusing, what referrals are appropriate, and what changes require urgent care.
