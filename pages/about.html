Meet [Name]

For many years, [Name] worked as a vision therapist, helping patients practice the visual skills involved in reading, tracking, focusing, eye teaming, spatial awareness, and everyday function.

That work taught her that people can have excellent eyesight and still struggle with how efficiently their visual system operates. It also taught her how often effort is mistaken for ability. A person may complete a task while using so much concentration, tension, avoidance, or recovery time that the cost remains invisible to everyone around them.

Living with Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, hypermobility, and other chronic health conditions deepened that understanding. Bodies do not exist in separate departments. Pain can affect attention. Fatigue can change tolerance for visual demand. Joint instability can complicate posture and stamina. Sensory environments can become harder to navigate when several systems are already working overtime.

Between Sight & Shadow grew from a desire to make that complexity easier to understand. It is not a replacement for optometry, physical therapy, occupational therapy, mental health care, or medical diagnosis. It is an educational space for noticing patterns, reducing unnecessary strain, preparing better questions, and finding accommodations that respect fluctuating bodies.

What you will find here

You will find accessible explanations of functional vision, practical approaches to visual and sensory strain, low-energy spiritual practices, printable tools, and reflections from someone who has stood on both sides of the helping relationship.

The goal is not to hand you a single answer. It is to help you become a more informed witness to your own experience, while knowing when a symptom deserves evaluation from a qualified professional.
