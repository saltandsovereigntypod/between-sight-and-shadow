Meet Kate

A bridge between years of vision-therapy experience, life in a hypermobile body, and a spiritual path rooted in autonomy.

The person behind the threshold

Seeing the whole person

For many years, Kate worked as a vision therapist, helping patients practice visual skills involved in reading, tracking, focusing, eye teaming, spatial awareness, and everyday function.

Her work now brings together professional experience, life in a hypermobile body, and a spiritual practice centered on autonomy, accessibility, and embodied self-trust.

Kate’s years in vision therapy taught her that clear eyesight does not always mean comfortable or efficient vision. A person may finish a task while using intense concentration, physical tension, avoidance, or long recovery periods. The result can look successful from the outside while carrying a cost no one else can see.

Living with Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, hypermobility, and other chronic health conditions deepened that understanding. Bodies do not operate as unrelated departments. Pain can affect attention. Fatigue can reduce tolerance for visual demand. Joint instability can influence posture and stamina. Sensory environments may become harder to navigate when several systems are already working overtime.

Between Sight & Shadow grew from a desire to make those overlapping experiences easier to understand. It is an educational space for noticing patterns, reducing unnecessary strain, preparing better questions, and exploring accommodations that respect fluctuating bodies.

A spiritual practice without performance

Kate is also a witch and devotee of Lilith. Her spiritual work centers autonomy, embodied consent, honest anger, self-possession, and the right to refuse systems that demand self-abandonment. Spirituality is offered here as a source of reflection, ritual, meaning, and personal agency, never as a substitute for healthcare.

Lilith’s history is layered and contested. Kate’s practice reflects a contemporary devotional relationship and personal theology while acknowledging that historical Jewish traditions, medieval folklore, and modern occult interpretations are not interchangeable.

What you will find here

You will find accessible explanations of functional vision, practical approaches to visual and sensory strain, low-energy spiritual practices, researched essays, and The Body Ledger, a private tracking and appointment-preparation tool currently available as a front-end demonstration.

Professional description

Kate is a former vision therapist, educator, chronic-illness advocate, and spiritual practitioner. She shares general education and lived-experience tools. She does not diagnose conditions or provide optometric vision therapy through this website.
